🗺️ How to use these scenarios — the 4-TF Top-Down Workflow

The candlestick scenarios below are step 5 of 6. Don't drop to 5M and start hunting for sweep→CHoCH until steps 1-4 are green. Most losing trades happen because the trader skipped to 5M too early.
1
📊 4H BIAS
DO: Open the 4H chart. Higher highs + higher lows = bullish. Lower highs + lower lows = bearish. Sideways = NEUTRAL = no trade today.
One word in your head — "BUY today" or "SELL today" or "skip"
▼
2
🎯 1H STRUCTURE + POI
DO: Drop to 1H. Confirm 1H structure agrees with 4H. Mark the last unmitigated demand OB (for buys) or supply OB (for sells). Mark any 1H FVG that hasn't been closed.
A horizontal line on your chart marking the 1H zone — your "destination"
▼
3
📍 15M ZONE + ROADMAP
DO: Drop to 15M. Check the indicator's roadmap layer (AUTO-LONDON / AUTO-NY / MANUAL boxes). Is your 1H POI inside it? Is zone status CONFIRMED or BREAKER on the indicator dashboard?
"Price is approaching the zone" or "Far away — set alert and walk away"
▼
4
⏰ KILLZONE GATE BLOCKING
DO: Check current ET time. Asia 20:00-01:00 / London 02:00-05:00 / NY AM 09:30-12:00 / NY PM 13:30-16:00. If outside any killzone, STOP HERE. No matter how good the setup looks, rule 8.2 says no trade.
"Inside killzone, continue" or "Off-hours, set alert + walk away"
▼
5
⭐ 5M SWEEP → CHoCH ★ THE 5 SCENARIOS BELOW
DO: NOW drop to 5M (not before). Watch for sweep of a key level (Asian H/L, PDH/PDL, equal H/L) inside the 1H zone. Within 5 bars of the sweep, watch for CHoCH (close above swing high for buys, close below swing low for sells).
Entry trigger fires on the exact 5M bar where sweep + CHoCH align — the scenarios below show what this looks like in real candles
▼
6
✅ SCORE + RR + BOT VERDICT
DO: Read indicator score (out of /18) and HTF count (out of /6). Calculate RR — must be ≥ 1:3. Calculate position size for 1% risk. DM @seyilisto_v2_bot with the full setup. Bot replies TAKE / SKIP / WAIT in 7-15s.
If TAKE → place order with exact SL / TP1 / TP2. If SKIP → log the reason in journal. If WAIT → re-check in 5-10 bars.
⚠️ Why students fail: They open 5M too early, see a sweep, get excited, enter, and lose. Sweep→CHoCH on 5M is meaningless without 4H bias agreeing, the 1H zone aligned, and the killzone open. The pattern is the trigger, not the setup. The setup is everything in steps 1-4. Drill this order into your students before you let them touch the 5M timeframe.
