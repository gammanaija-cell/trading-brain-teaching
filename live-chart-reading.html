★Visual Glossary — The 3 Boxes That Matter

🎓 Student-level: what each colored box on your chart means

When you load v5.5 on a chart, you'll see three different types of coloured boxes. They look similar but mean very different things. Knowing the difference is the difference between watching the right level and entering the wrong one.

📍 BOX 1 — The Roadmap Zone

PURPLE · DASHED · STRATEGY
What it is: A "look here" area, drawn automatically at session open (London or NY). Meaning: Price might come here looking for liquidity. NOT an entry signal — just a heads-up zone. Drawn when: 02:00 ET (London open) or 09:30 ET (NY open). Anchored to Asian range or London/Asian range respectively. What to do: Watch this zone. When price arrives, drop to 5M and wait for trigger. The roadmap is the radar, not the trigger. Don't: Enter just because price reached the roadmap. The roadmap says "here's where action might happen" — you still need a sweep + CHoCH inside it. Roadmap na the "watch here" map — drawn before session start. E no be entry signal, na "where to look." Wait till price reach there, then drop to 5M check for sweep + CHoCH.

🟢 BOX 2 — The Buy Zone

GREEN · SOLID · TACTICAL
What it is: A trigger zone drawn AT a bullish order block candle (a candle where buyers stepped in hard). Meaning: If sweep + CHoCH fires inside this box, the system thinks long is favoured. Status labels: "Waiting CHOCH" = trigger pending. "✓ CONTINUATION" = trigger fired, evaluate now. What to do: Read the score badge. If A+/A++ tier AND in killzone AND conviction OK → this is your long entry zone. Don't: Enter just because the green box appeared. The box exists because an OB formed; the TRIGGER (sweep + CHoCH) has to fire INSIDE the box. Buy zone na where you enter long — but only if the score qualify, killzone open, and CHoCH fire inside the box. Box alone no be signal.

🔴 BOX 3 — The Sell Zone

RED · SOLID · TACTICAL
What it is: A trigger zone drawn AT a bearish order block candle (a candle where sellers stepped in hard). Meaning: If sweep + CHoCH fires inside this box, the system thinks short is favoured. Status labels: Same as buy zone — "Waiting CHOCH" or "✓ CONTINUATION". What to do: Read score → if qualifies (A+/A++ tier, killzone open, conviction OK) → this is your short entry zone. Don't: Enter on the red box alone. Same rule as buy zone — the trigger (sweep + CHoCH) has to fire INSIDE the box. Sell zone work same way as buy zone, just opposite direction. Red box for short, green box for long. Box no be entry — sweep + CHoCH inside na the entry.

🎯 How the 3 boxes work together (the workflow)

Step 1 — ROADMAP appears: At session open, system draws purple dashed Roadmap zone(s). This is your "watch list." Walk away if price isn't near roadmap yet. Step 2 — Price approaches Roadmap: Price reaches purple zone. Now you start paying attention. Drop to 5M. Step 3 — BUY/SELL Zone forms: Within (or near) the Roadmap, a green Buy Zone or red Sell Zone draws when an OB candle prints. Status starts as "Waiting CHOCH." Step 4 — Trigger fires: Sweep + CHoCH happens inside the Buy/Sell Zone. Status flips to "✓ CONTINUATION." Now check score badge. Step 5 — Score qualifies: If badge shows ≥9/18 AND killzone open AND conviction multiplier OK → take the trade. → Roadmap = STRATEGY (where to look). Buy/Sell zones = TACTICS (where to enter). Without roadmap context, zones are random. Without zones, roadmap is just a region. 3 step easy: (1) Roadmap show "watch this area" (2) Price reach roadmap, then Buy or Sell zone form (3) Sweep+CHoCH happen inside the zone, score qualify → enter. No skip steps.

4The Dual-Pass Score Rule — Read the Badge Twice

🎯 The badge means different things on 15M vs 5M — read it twice

v5.2 runs on whatever chart you have open. So the score badge on a 15M chart reflects 15M-bar confluence; the same badge on a 5M chart reflects 5M-bar confluence. Two different reads, two different jobs. Pass 1 — on the 15M chart: "Is this zone worth my attention?" If 15M score < 9, SKIP entirely. Never even drop to 5M. The 15M is your gate. Pass 2 — on the 5M chart: "Is the trigger worth pulling right now?" Only meaningful if 15M already passed. The 5M is your trigger.

⚙️ Optimization Notes (things easy to miss)

HTF Y/6 is timeframe-invariant. The HTF count looks UP at 4H + 1H regardless of which chart you're on. If 15M reads HTF 5/6, the 5M will also read HTF 5/6. Only the score number changes between timeframes — HTF count stays the same. So if you see HTF 3/6 on 15M, dropping to 5M won't magically improve it. Score velocity matters more than absolute score. A score that JUMPED from 7 → 14 in one bar (sweep + CHoCH just fired) is fresher and stronger than a score that's been sitting at 14 for 20 bars (institutional interest fading). When you switch to a chart with a "good" score, glance back 5 bars: was the score already that high, or did it just spike? Fresh spike = stronger signal. Indicator cache quirk. When you switch chart timeframe (15M ↔ 5M), Pine occasionally doesn't recompute zones. If the score looks suspiciously identical to the other TF, right-click the indicator → "Recompile" to force a fresh calculation. (v5.2 limitation; v5.3 will auto-refresh.) The "perfect storm" configuration. Bump size to full 1% without hesitation. These show up 2-4 times a week, max. Common mistake to avoid. Reading the badge only on 5M because that's where the action looks crisp. The 5M badge is the LAST gate, not the first. Always 15M first. If you don't enforce that order, you'll repeatedly take 5M-only "A++" setups that have no HTF backing and watch them reverse on you within 4 bars. Don't double-count the trigger. When 5M flips to ✓ CONTINUATION after a sweep, don't go back to 15M and refresh — wait the 15M out. The 15M zone should still say "Waiting CHOCH" or already-confirmed BEFORE the 5M trigger. 5What's Actually INSIDE a Zone — Coded vs Manual

📐 Two Zone Layers in v5.2 (don't confuse them)

Layer 1 — Roadmap zones (bias map): Drawn at session start (London open or NY open). These are where to look, not where to enter. They are heuristic ATR boxes anchored to session H/L. Labeled ROADMAP BUY (AUTO-LONDON) / ROADMAP SELL (AUTO-NY). Layer 2 — Trigger zones (entry box): Drawn ONLY when sweep + CHoCH fires inside (or near) a roadmap zone. These are where to actually enter. They carry the score badge. Labeled BUY ZONE — Waiting CHOCH or BUY ZONE ✓ CONTINUATION. Relationship: Roadmap = "look here." Trigger = "enter here." The score badge lives on the TRIGGER zone, not the roadmap zone. Two type of zone dey. Roadmap na "where to watch" — drawn for morning, na guess sha. Trigger zone na "where to enter" — drawn when sweep + CHoCH happen, na the one with score number. No mix dem up.

✅ What v5.2 auto-detects (folded into the 18-point score)

Sweep detection — PDH / PDL / Asian H/L / session H/L / equal H/L CHoCH — structure flip after sweep within 5 bars Fair Value Gap (FVG) — inside or adjacent to the zone Order Block (OB) — last opposing candle before displacement EMA alignment — price vs fast/slow EMAs RSI extreme veto — BLOCK above 70 / below 30 Volume confirmation — only reliable on NQ (CME volume); gated for FX/gold HTF alignment count — 4H / 1H / 15M / 5M structural agreement (the Y/6 in the badge) Killzone gate — London / NY AM / NY PM / Asia time-windowing ATR-based zone width — volatility-adjusted box dimensions Session range capture — Asian / London / NY auto-anchored highs and lows

⚠️ What v5.2 does NOT see — you must add manually

Volume Profile (POC, HVN, LVN) — use TradingView's "Volume Profile Visible Range" or "Fixed Range Volume Profile" tool. Why: zone inside HVN = institutional accumulation = stronger hold. Zone at LVN = thin air = price slices through. 4H / Daily unmitigated POIs — manually mark HTF demand/supply OBs pre-session. (v5.3.1 spec includes an auto-scanner for this — not yet shipped.) Liquidity pools — equal highs / equal lows / trendline liquidity not yet swept. Mark with horizontal rays. News event marks — NFP / CPI / FOMC release timestamps from forexfactory. VWAP + std-dev bands — TradingView's built-in VWAP indicator (separate overlay). Daily / Weekly / Monthly opens — TradingView built-in tool. Order book / footprint / delta / cumulative volume delta — needs broker API or CME data add-on. Skip for paper-trading phase. Real institutional liquidity heat-maps — paid services only (Bookmap, NinjaTrader Order Flow). Out of scope.

🔍 Pre-Pass Zone Quality Checklist (verify BEFORE the dual-pass)

The 18-point score measures TRIGGER quality (the moment of execution). This 8-point checklist measures ZONE quality (the location of the trade). A high score in a low-quality zone = textbook bull trap. Run this checklist on the 15M chart before you read the score. If 6+ checks pass, the zone is high-quality. If <4, even a 14/18 score is suspect.
  1. Zone is FRESH — first touch > second touch > third touch. Each tap consumes orders.
  2. Inside or at HVN — high-volume node behind it = institutional accumulation. (manual — Volume Profile tool)
  3. NOT at LVN — low-volume node = thin air, price slices through. Avoid these.
  4. Confluence with 4H / Daily POI — HTF demand/supply OB sitting inside the 15M zone = compounded edge.
  5. Liquidity pool nearby — equal highs/lows above (sells) or below (buys) = institutions need to grab it. Marks the targets.
  6. Created by DISPLACEMENT, not slow drift — sharp move out of the level = aggressive participation. Slow consolidation = weak.
  7. Distance from POC — far from POC = trend continuation possible. Close to POC = mean-reversion magnet (avoid for breakout entries).
  8. Killzone-formed — zones created during London or NY AM are stronger than zones created in Asia.
→ Score = "should I pull the trigger?" · Checklist = "should this zone exist at all?" You need both. Score without checklist = trap. Checklist without score = patience without trigger. Score na trigger quality, but where the zone dey matter pass. Zone for HVN with HTF POI nearby + fresh first touch — even 11/18 score go work. Zone for LVN, no HTF backing, third touch — even 17/18 go disappoint. Always check zone quality first, score after.

⚠️ Honesty Caveat — the roadmap is heuristic, not gospel

v5.2's roadmap is built from ATR-buffered boxes anchored to session highs/lows (Asian H/L for London open, more extreme of London or Asian range for NY open). It is NOT a real institutional POI scanner. It does NOT read volume profile. It does NOT scan 4H/Daily order blocks. The boxes will sometimes draw at locations that have no actual liquidity behind them. Treat the roadmap as "first guess at session liquidity," not as validated institutional levels. This is precisely why the manual pre-pass checklist above matters. The score gate measures trigger cleanliness; your eyes have to validate the location quality. v5.3.1 spec (planned, not yet shipped): replaces the heuristic ATR anchor with a proper HTF order-block scanner that scans 4H / Daily for unmitigated demand and supply, then uses those as roadmap anchors instead of session-range high/low. Volume Profile integration (POC/HVN/LVN scoring) is on the v5.4 backlog. Until those ship, you do this validation manually. The roadmap box no be real institutional level — na ATR estimate around session H/L. E fit dey correct, e fit dey wrong. The score check trigger only — you must check zone location yourself with your eyes (POC, HVN, HTF POI). Two work — bot dey do trigger, you dey do zone validation.
