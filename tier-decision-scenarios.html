🛑 UNIVERSAL INVALIDATIONS · Apply to ALL Tiers

REGARDLESS OF TIER, THESE ALWAYS BLOCK A TRADE

🛑 Block #1 — 2 Daily Losses Already

✗ SKIP — RULE 5 (DAILY LIMIT)
Rule 5: 2 losses → stop trading for the day. Even if a clean A++ setup appears 30 min later — SKIP. The rule isn't "stop after 3" or "stop if down a lot" — it's exactly 2 losses, every time. This protects against revenge trading and tilt cascades. Your edge in trade #3 is statistically lower than trades 1-2 because emotional state degrades. Two loss don dey daily limit. No matter wetin you see for chart, close am. Even if A++ pop up, leave am. Tomorrow go come — your money go still dey there. Today don finish, take rest.

🛑 Block #2 — News Event Within 30 min

✗ SKIP — RULE 4
Rule 4: No entries within 30 minutes of NFP / CPI / FOMC / FOMC minutes. News spikes break technical structure — even institutional levels get blown through Spreads widen 5-20× during release. Even if your trade direction is "right," slippage destroys RR Wait at least 30 min AFTER the release for the initial reaction to settle News time, market dey craze. CPI, NFP, FOMC — even sharp setup go fly through your stop loss. Spread go wide like Lagos pothole. Wait small after the news, market go calm down.

🛑 Block #3 — Both Bull AND Bear Zones Active

✗ SKIP — RULE 4 (UNDECIDED MARKET)
Rule 4 (skip list): "Both bull and bear zones active simultaneously = market undecided." When both demand and supply OBs are active and unmitigated, price oscillates between them Either side could "win" — until one zone gets decisively broken, no directional bias exists Trading either side here = 50/50 coin flip with spread cost — negative expectancy Buyers and sellers dey fight pass. Two zones active, market dey waka up and down. Until one side win, you dey gamble. Wait till market choose direction, then trade that side. No be lottery.

🛑 Block #4 — Indicator Shows RSI BLOCK

✗ SKIP — DASHBOARD RED FLAG
Rule 4: "RSI cell shows BLOCK" → no trade. The Pine v5.2 dashboard shows multiple confluence cells. RSI BLOCK fires when RSI is in extreme territory (overbought for buys, oversold for sells) Means the move is exhausted — buying near tops or selling near bottoms = mean reversion against you Even with a 14/18 A++-eligible setup, RSI BLOCK overrides — wait for RSI to reset Even if everything else perfect, when RSI dey extreme, the move don tire. Buying for top or selling for bottom — na the way to lose money. Wait small, RSI go cool down, then enter.
