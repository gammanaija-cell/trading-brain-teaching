1What Makes a BUY ZONE Form

🟢 The exact Pine v5.5.2 logic that creates a green Buy Zone

Condition 1: Previous bar is bearish — close[1] < open[1] Condition 2: Current bar is a strong bullish impulse:
  • close > open (green candle)
  • (close − open) > (high − low) × 0.55 (body is > 55% of total range — fat candle)
  • close > close[1] (closed above prior bar's close)
 Condition 3: Bar is confirmed (closed) — barstate.isconfirmed. No intra-bar zone drawing. Condition 4: The "Show Order Block Zones" input is enabled (default ON). NOT REQUIRED: Sweep, CHoCH, killzone, HTF alignment. The zone forms purely from the 2-bar candle pattern. Those other factors come in later via the score and conviction layers. → The zone box is drawn from the prior bearish candle's body (open[1] = top, close[1] = bottom). That's the "imbalance" institutions left behind. Zone form when bearish candle suddenly get answered by big green impulse candle. The system mark the bearish candle's body as the zone. No need sweep, no need CHoCH for the box to draw — those come later when system check tier and conviction.

🔴 SELL ZONE — same logic, mirrored

Condition 1: Previous bar is bullish — close[1] > open[1] Condition 2: Current bar is strong bearish impulse — close < open + body > 55% range + close < close[1] Condition 3: Bar confirmed. Box drawn: from prior bullish candle's body (open[1] = bottom of zone, close[1] = top).

📊 Status: "Waiting CHOCH" vs "✓ CONTINUATION"

"Waiting CHOCH" — zone exists but structure is not yet in confirmed uptrend (HH+HL pattern). Indicator is waiting for break-of-structure to validate the bias. "✓ CONTINUATION" — bull_choch fires while price is inside or near the zone OR zone formed during an existing uptrend. NOW the trigger pattern is "live" — read score and conviction to decide. → Box appearing = there's a candle pattern. Box flipping to ✓ = the structural confirmation. Don't enter until ✓.

Important: The VP histogram is computed from bars on the CURRENT chart's timeframe. So a "Daily" period on a 1M chart computes the profile from ~390 1-minute bars; on a 5M chart from ~78 5-minute bars; on a 1H chart from ~24 1-hour bars. The MORE bars feeding the histogram, the SMOOTHER the result — but also the LESS reactive to recent price. Common mistake: Using "Weekly" on a 1M chart pulls 1500+ bars (max cap) — you're seeing 25 hours of micro-data crammed into one profile. Useful for context, but lacks today's detail. Use "Session" or "Daily" instead. Choose VP period to match your chart timeframe: 1M chart → Session/Daily VP. 5M → Daily/2-Day. 15M → 2-Day/Weekly. Higher TF chart, longer VP period. Wrong combo = either too smooth (no detail) or too noisy (no structure).
