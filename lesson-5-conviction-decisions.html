🎯 Lesson 5 — Conviction-Aware Decisions

When to override the score · When to stand down · 8 worked scenarios

📚 The new rule v5.5.2 enforces

You used to read just the score badge (e.g., "14/18 = A++ → take"). That worked. But it ignored context — was the trade aligned with the day's regime? Was price extended? Was the market more volatile than the options market expected?

v5.5.2 adds a conviction multiplier on top of the score. The multiplier ranges 0.4 to 2.5 and modulates 4 independent context factors:

  • Location (VP) — is the zone at VAL/HVN/POC?
  • Regime (AMT) — is your trade with or against today's auction type?
  • Probability (SD position) — has price already moved most of its expected range?
  • Volatility (Realized vs Implied) — is the market matching, exceeding, or undershooting expectations?

Conviction = base score × multiplier_product. The tier (A++/A+/B+) still drives go/no-go, but conviction now drives position size and overrides.
